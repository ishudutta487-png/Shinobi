# Shinobi
a trusty workspace companion designed to bring a little bit of gaming flair and lightweight utility to the Hack Club Slack. Inspired by the stealth, precision, and lore of Sekiro

this bot sits quietly in the background until called upon, ready to slice through the daily grind with a quick response. It's built to be fast, fun, and a great way to add some personality to your channels

Shinobi comes equipped with a handful of commands to keep things interesting. You can test its reflexes using the /shinobi-ping command, or ask it to lighten the mood with /shinobi-joke and /shinobi-catfact when you need a quick break from coding. But its true specialty lies in the /shinobi-quote command, which drops iconic, hard-hitting wisdom directly from the world of Sekiro to remind you and your friends that "hesitation is defeat."
